Design a professional, premium, modern website for an AI technology company named:

VELQUORIN LABS

PROJECT PURPOSE

This is the official corporate and marketing website for Velquorin Labs.

The production website will be built using Next.js, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, and the Next.js App Router.

The website will eventually be deployed at:

velquorinlabs.com

Velquorin Labs develops AI-powered software and automation solutions for businesses.

Current and planned core services include:

1. AI Chatbots
2. AI Automation
3. Conversational AI Forms
4. Custom AI Solutions

The company is also developing SHASHA AI, an intelligent business chatbot platform.

Future Velquorin Labs products may expand into AI-assisted email management, business operations, workflow automation, lead management, integrations, and mobile applications.

The website design therefore must be flexible enough to support additional AI products and services later.

--------------------------------------------------

BRAND PERSONALITY

The brand should feel:

• Premium
• Intelligent
• Modern
• Innovative
• Professional
• Trustworthy
• Technical without being intimidating
• Suitable for business clients
• Suitable for international and US-based customers

Do NOT make the website look like:

• a cryptocurrency website
• a gaming website
• a cyberpunk website
• an AI-generated template
• an overly futuristic concept site
• a generic SaaS landing page
• a website overloaded with neon effects
• a website full of robot imagery

The result should feel like a real, established AI software and technology company.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use a clean modern sans-serif visual style similar to Geist.

Typography should have:

• strong large headings
• excellent readability
• clear hierarchy
• restrained letter spacing
• comfortable paragraph widths
• professional SaaS/technology styling

Avoid decorative or futuristic fonts for normal content.

--------------------------------------------------

GLOBAL LAYOUT

Use a maximum content width of approximately 1200px.

Use generous spacing and whitespace.

The layout should feel spacious rather than crowded.

Sections should have clear visual separation while maintaining a continuous premium experience.

The website must be fully responsive for:

• Desktop
• Laptop
• Tablet
• Mobile

Design mobile intentionally rather than simply shrinking the desktop layout.

ANIMATION

Use tasteful micro-interactions.

Examples:

• gentle card hover
• subtle button movement
• soft gradient movement
• restrained glow
• subtle scroll reveal
• small workflow animations

Animations should feel premium and professional.

Avoid:

• excessive parallax
• constantly moving backgrounds
• distracting particles
• flashing neon
• aggressive 3D animation

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

--------------------------------------------------

ACCESSIBILITY

Design with accessibility in mind.

Requirements:

• readable contrast
• visible focus states
• keyboard-friendly controls
• semantic heading hierarchy
• sufficiently large buttons
• accessible navigation
• comfortable mobile tap targets
• do not rely exclusively on color to communicate meaning

--------------------------------------------------

SEO

The design should support semantic content structure.

Use:

One clear H1 per page.

Logical H2 and H3 hierarchy.

Content sections that can contain descriptive text.

Do not place important SEO text exclusively inside graphics.
